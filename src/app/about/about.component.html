ABOUT DRELLO

A simpler way to get things done.

Work feels better when everything has its place. Drello helps you organize tasks, manage priorities, and keep moving toward your goals.

OUR PURPOSE

Less clutter. More clarity.

Drello is a task management application built around a simple idea: organizing your work should not be complicated. A clear overview makes it easier to understand what needs to be done, what is in progress, and what you have already accomplished.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Everything you need to stay organized.

From a simple to-do list to sprints with a whole team.

Organize with boards

Group your work into columns and cards, and drag tasks forward as they progress.

See your schedule

Find every deadline on a calendar, and see how tasks spread out on a timeline.

Plan in sprints

Fill a backlog, plan sprints, and follow the progress until they are done.

Work together

Invite people to a board, and see their changes on your screen as they happen.

Automate the routine

Let rules handle repetitive steps, like marking a card done when it reaches Done.

Make it your own

Start from templates, add your own fields, and set priorities and labels.
